# Global Impact & Deployments

## Ghana Cocoa Campaign

During the Lacuna Fund Climate & Forests project (running from September 2024 to
March 2025), Ground served as the primary field mapping tool for a consortium
including CERSGIS, SERVIR West Africa, the University of Ghana, and the World
Resources Institute (WRI). A team of approximately 30 enumerators—consisting of
CERSGIS staff, University of Ghana students, and YouthMappers from the
University of Ghana and University of Cape Coast—mapped **21,031 cocoa
polygons**.

This effort gathered critical plot-level reference data, including management
and shade tree attributes, which were subsequently used to train and validate
Forest Data Partnership (FDaP) cocoa models and support Google's Sustainable
Supply Chain (SuSo) initiatives. To support open science and data sharing, a
subset of this high-quality field data was published on Zenodo under a CC-BY 4.0
license, receiving **over 2,000 views and 280 downloads**.

## Burundi Coffee Registry

In Burundi, Ground supported a massive smallholder registration campaign led by
the Association pour l'Entraide et le Développement Communautaire (ADISCO) and
the International Trade Centre (ITC) between May and August 2025. Field crews
successfully mapped **over 30,000 coffee plots** as of August 2025. This project
demonstrated Ground's capacity to operate under extreme connectivity
constraints, enabling rapid registration of coffee growers to secure access to
global markets.

## Kenya Coffee Pilots & Cooperative Success

Under pilots led by Agrisolutions Enterprises Ltd (ASEL) and funded by the
Palladium Group and ITC, **6,000 coffee plots** were mapped in Kenya by
mid-2025.

Building on this momentum, a training session conducted on May 29, 2025, by the
FAO trained representatives from local cooperatives in Nandi County. Four
qualified participants were selected to design and coordinate their own
campaigns. Within two months, these cooperatives mapped **over 2,000 additional
plots** independently, achieving roughly **60% mapping coverage** of their
cooperative societies:

*   **Toroton Farmers Cooperative Society (FCS)**: Mapped **1,400 out of 2,100**
    total plots.
*   **Meteitei Set Kobor FCS**: Mapped **400 out of 700** total plots.

## Viet Nam EUDR Pilots

To meet strict EUDR regulatory requirements, active Training of Trainers (ToT)
and georeferencing campaigns are underway in Viet Nam, led by the FAO alongside
forest rangers, local companies (such as Duc Hien Company Limited and the Quang
Tri Trading Corporation), and agricultural cooperatives (including Bon Phuong,
Phat Loc, and Hiep Phat).

These active pilots have mapped plot-level georeferenced data for key
forest-risk commodities:

*   **Coffee**: 41 plots georeferenced.
*   **Rubber**: 25 plots georeferenced.
*   **Timber**: 38 plots georeferenced.

## Capacity Building & Training

Ground has systematically expanded its capacity-building outreach across more
than **30 completed training sessions globally**. This includes major regional
workshops:

*   **Nigeria**: 120 trainees participating in a national forestry monitoring
    session.
*   **Nandi County, Kenya**: 111 cooperative representatives trained in advanced
    survey creation.
*   **Embu County, Kenya**: 65 ministerial staff and cooperative union leaders
    trained to deploy surveys across multiple counties.

## Pipeline Expansion

The demand for Ground 2.0’s structured, map-first features is reflected in a
rapidly expanding institutional pipeline:

*   **DRSRS Kenya**: A planned coffee georeferencing campaign led by the
    Directorate of Resource Surveys and Remote Sensing (DRSRS) is backed by a
    finalized **$100,000 Letter of Agreement (LoA)**.
*   **Sierra Leone**: The Produce and Monitoring Board (SL/PMB), in
    collaboration with the European Forest Institute (EFI), is planning a pilot
    mapping cocoa plots, building on an initial trial that collected 150 points.
*   **Cameroon**: Proposed partnerships with the Conseil Interprofessionel du
    Café et Cacao (CICC) and EFI are set to georeference coffee and cocoa
    parcels starting in late 2026.
